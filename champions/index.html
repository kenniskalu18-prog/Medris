Why Become a Champion?

Being a Mobihealth Campus Champion is more than a title — it's a launchpad.

Make an Impact

Promote health awareness, encourage healthier choices and contribute to meaningful change on campus.

Build Your Skills

Develop communication, leadership, teamwork, advocacy and project-management skills.

Earn Recognition

Who Can Apply?

Students currently studying at the University of Lagos, from any faculty and any level.

  • Students from ALL LEVELS on UNILAG campus
  • Undergraduate students, any faculty or department
  • Passionate about healthcare, tech, communication, advocacy or community impact
  • Willing to actively represent and promote Mobihealth on campus
