Why Join

Why Become a Champion?

Being a Mobihealth Campus Champion is more than a title — it's a launchpad.

Make an Impact

Promote health awareness, encourage healthier choices and contribute to meaningful change on campus.

Build Your Skills

Develop communication, leadership, teamwork, advocacy and project-management skills.

Earn Recognition

Gain certificates, recognition and opportunities to grow through your involvement.

Enjoy Exclusive Perks

Access selected Mobihealth activities, training opportunities, events and other benefits.

Join a Community

Connect with passionate students and become part of a growing campus health movement.

What to Expect

Choose Activities That Match Your Strengths

Every Champion contributes differently — pick the mix that fits your interests.

Social Media Marketing

Support campaigns across student social platforms and communities.

TikTok, Reels & Content Creation

Create fun, relatable health and telehealth content for Gen Z.

Campus Outreaches

Take telehealth to halls, faculties, departments and events.

Health Campaigns & Screenings

Support health awareness, screenings, telehealth demos and specialist campaigns.

Student Onboarding

Show students how to access doctors and specialists from their phones.

Research & Development

Participate in digital health, telehealth and AI research, surveys and impact studies.

Innovation

Identify real healthcare challenges on campus and pitch promising ideas.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply?

Students currently enrolled at any university or tertiary institution, from any faculty and any level.

  • Students from ALL LEVELS, at any university or tertiary institution
  • Undergraduate students, any faculty or department
  • Passionate about healthcare, tech, communication, advocacy or community impact
  • Willing to actively represent and promote Mobihealth on campus
