Delete your Levromart account

How to ask for your Levromart account and its data to be deleted.

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Levromart and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and open the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Enter your password, type delete my account, then tap Delete permanently.

Before you start: if your Levromart wallet still has money, withdraw it or use it first. A shop (vendor) account also needs to have no orders still in progress and no unpaid commission.

Can't sign in?

Message Levromart on WhatsApp at 0806 322 7669 from the phone number on your account, say "Delete my account", and give your name and the email or phone number you signed up with. We will confirm it is you and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in details and account picture.
  • Your shop, listings, packages and requests, if you have a shop.
  • Your orders, messages, conversations, saved items, cart, follows, notifications and Levi assistant chats.
  • Your wallet record and your points.

What may be kept

  • When an account is deleted, Levromart keeps a protected backup copy for a limited period so that a deletion made by mistake can be undone. Only Levromart administrators can open it, and it is never shown to other users.
  • Records of payments and refunds may be kept for as long as the law or a dispute requires.
  • Reviews, messages or order records that other people hold in their own accounts are not changed by you deleting your account.

Deleting an account cannot be undone from the app. See the Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
