Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Levromart is an online marketplace that connects buyers with independent vendors, mostly students and small businesses around university campuses and cities across Nigeria. Listings include products, services, rentals, packages/bundles, healthcare equipment, food, electronics and more, for both rental and outright purchase. By creating an account or using Levromart, you agree to these terms.

1. What Levromart is (and isn't)

Levromart is a marketplace and transaction-facilitation platform. We provide the technology, vendor onboarding/verification, payment and order records, messaging, and dispute process. We are not the seller or renter of record for a vendor's listing. Every product, service, or package is supplied by the independent vendor shown on the listing, and that vendor is responsible for the legality, authenticity, condition, safety, availability, accuracy, and fulfilment of what they offer.

Vendor verification means that Levromart has completed the verification checks required for that account; it is not a guarantee that every future transaction or listing by that vendor will be problem-free.

Verification badges: a green tick beside a vendor's name means Levromart has verified them as a student of the campus shown; a blue tick means Levromart has verified them as a seller who is not a student (for example, a seller based on or near a campus). A paid premium (gold) verification may be introduced later; these terms will be updated before it is.

Only categories that currently have live listings from verified vendors are shown when you browse by category; the marketplace, its promotions and its search results change as vendors add or remove listings.

2. Accounts

You must provide accurate information when registering. A phone number and password are required; an email address is optional. You're responsible for keeping your password secure and for all activity under your account. Vendor accounts additionally require identity/business verification before their storefront goes live. Student vendors may be asked for proof such as a student ID card or biodata page; vendors who are not students can say so, and we may ask for other proof of identity. Shop teams. A vendor may invite managers or staff (who must have their own Levromart account and must accept the invitation first) to work on the shop's orders and chats. Team members can see that shop's orders, including customers' names and delivery details once an order is accepted, and act for the shop on them; they cannot see or change the shop's bank details, wallet, earnings or settings. The vendor is responsible for who they add and for what their team does, and can remove a team member at any time. Customers deal with the shop, whose name is shown.

3. Orders, rentals & payments

  • Every order on Levromart is paid through Levromart's secure checkout: with your Levromart wallet, or through Paystack (card, bank transfer or USSD). Levromart never sees or stores your card details. Please do not pay a vendor outside the app: a payment made outside Levromart's checkout (cash, or a transfer straight to the vendor) is not held or protected by Levromart, and Levromart cannot refund it or decide a dispute about it. An order is only treated as paid once Paystack confirms the payment, or once it is paid from your Levromart wallet. Paying from your wallet has no Paystack fee; the shop is paid and Levromart's commission is taken exactly as for a card payment.
  • Delivery fees can depend on the campus. A vendor can set free delivery, a flat fee or a fee per kilometre for different campuses. When you choose delivery, choose the campus where you will receive the order: the delivery fee is worked out for that campus. If your delivery address is not on the campus you chose, the vendor may decline or cancel the order, or ask you to switch to pickup. When you order several items from the same shop in one order, delivery is charged once for that shop (the highest delivery fee among the items), not once per item.
  • Orders are paid in full before delivery. The money you pay is collected by Paystack and kept in Levromart's Paystack balance (Levromart's single account for collecting, paying out and refunding money); it is not sent to the vendor until the order is done. Once the payment is confirmed and the vendor marks the order out for delivery or handed over, you have 24 hours to confirm receipt or raise a dispute; if you do neither, the order completes automatically and the vendor's share is added to the vendor's wallet.
  • Vendors add their payout bank account in the app so they can be paid, and customers add a bank account in their own name to withdraw money from their Levromart wallet. Whoever adds an account (vendor or customer) is responsible for making sure the details are correct and that the account is their own. Levromart is not responsible for any payout, withdrawal or refund sent to an account that was entered wrongly or does not belong to the person it was meant for, and such money may not be recoverable.
  • Levromart's 3% commission is collected automatically the moment an order is paid: it belongs to Levromart straight away and does not wait for the buyer to confirm anything. The vendor's share (the other 97%) shows as the shop's earnings, and only that share waits for the buyer's confirmation and the 24-hour check; the money itself stays in Levromart's Paystack balance. A vendor moves earnings into their Levromart wallet (or withdraws them to their payout bank account): money paid for 24 hours or more with no open dispute moves straight away, and newer money only after Levromart approves the request. A vendor can choose which earnings to move, and must write a short reason when asking for money that is less than 24 hours old; Levromart may decline it and will give the reason, and the vendor can cancel the request while it is waiting. Levromart may decline a withdrawal request and will give the reason. If a buyer is refunded for an order, the amount refunded is taken off the shop's wallet automatically: a refund is paid out of the shop's share of the sale (what is left after Levromart's commission), and Levromart's 3% commission is never refunded or taken back: a refund always comes out of the shop's 97%. A refund can be requested until the order is completed. Within 24 hours of placing the order Paystack can return the money by itself; after 24 hours it may no longer be able to, so the shop or Levromart decides how it is paid back (for example into your Levromart wallet) (a dispute can still be raised if what arrived is not what was ordered). Levromart's 3% commission is taken the moment an order is paid and is never refunded. Money from an order with an open dispute is held until the dispute is settled. Amounts under ₦100 (the smallest amount Paystack can send) are always checked by Levromart first. For security, nothing can be withdrawn to a bank for 24 hours after a payout bank account is added or changed, and the first withdrawal after a change is checked and approved by Levromart before it is sent. This protects you if someone gets into your account and changes your bank details. Transfers usually arrive within minutes, but can take longer when a bank or Paystack is slow. Paystack passes card payments to Levromart about one business day after the buyer pays, so a payout for a recent card payment is sent on the next business day, automatically, once that money arrives.
  • Levromart wallet: every account has a Levromart wallet and an @tag. Money comes into it when you add it with Paystack (Paystack's fee is added on top), when someone sends it to your @tag, when you are refunded, and (for shops) when you move your earnings in. You can pay for orders with it, send it to another person's @tag, or withdraw it to a bank account in your own name. The money in all wallets is held by Levromart in its Paystack account; your wallet shows how much of it is yours. It earns no interest. Check the name and @tag before you send money: Levromart cannot reverse a transfer you made by mistake. Limits apply: at least ₦100 and at most ₦50,000 per transfer and ₦100,000 sent per day; top-ups of up to ₦100,000 at a time, up to a balance of ₦300,000 from top-ups; up to ten withdrawals a day. Your wallet PIN: every account has a 6-digit wallet PIN that you choose yourself. It is needed for every payment, transfer, withdrawal and top-up from your wallet, and it can be changed in Settings with your account password. Five wrong tries lock it for 15 minutes. You are responsible for your PIN in the same way you are responsible for the PIN of your bank account: keep it to yourself, never share it, and do not let anyone watch you enter it. Levromart staff, admins and support will never ask for your PIN, for any reason. Levromart is not responsible for any loss caused by a PIN that was shared, guessed, written down where others could find it, or otherwise not kept safe. Fees: your first 3 orders paid with your wallet are free. After that, paying for an order, sending money to another person and withdrawing to a bank each carry a fee. The fee is shown to you before you confirm, is taken from your wallet on top of the amount, and belongs to Levromart. Adding money with Paystack carries Paystack's own fee. A fee is given back if a withdrawal does not reach your bank. When you pay an order from your wallet, the order amount is shared as it is for every other payment: 97% goes to the shop and 3% to Levromart as commission; a refund is paid out of the shop's 97%. You can withdraw to your bank at any time with your PIN, including straight after you add or change your bank account, because the PIN is your safeguard. The only wait is for a shop's earnings: money a shop was paid in the last 24 hours can be moved or withdrawn only after those 24 hours, or earlier if Levromart approves it. Levromart may put a wallet on hold, decline a withdrawal (giving the reason), or reverse a movement where it suspects fraud, a stolen card, or a breach of these terms. An account with money in its wallet cannot be deleted until the money is withdrawn or used. A shop that withdraws its earnings to its bank bears Paystack's charge for sending the money, which is taken from each payout. Adding or changing a payout or refund account needs your account password.

4. Messaging, calls & chat history

  • Customers and vendors can message each other only while they have an accepted order together. A vendor cannot see a customer's name until the order is accepted.
  • In-app voice calls are available in the same conversations. Calls run through the Levromart app and your internet connection; your phone number is not shown to the other person. Levromart does not record call audio.
  • When an order is completed, the conversation stays open for 24 hours (30 minutes after a cancelled order) and then closes, unless you allow a shop's request to message you again (for 24 hours): it leaves your chat list and can no longer be opened or continued, so the two sides do not keep communicating outside an order. The messages are not deleted. They are kept securely as a record for disputes, fraud prevention and legal requirements, and Levromart administrators can review them when needed. If you later place a new order with the same vendor, the conversation reopens with its earlier messages.
  • Keep conversations and calls about the order. Do not use them to harass anyone, to share personal phone numbers, or to move a sale off Levromart to avoid commission or payment protections. Levromart administrators may review a conversation when a report or dispute is raised, and may restrict messaging or calling for accounts that break these rules.
  • Calls need microphone permission. You can allow incoming-call notifications in your device and Levromart settings; if they are off you may miss a call.
  • If an order cancels by itself because the shop did not confirm in time, the shop may send the customer a short message, at most three times for that order, (in the app and, if you gave a WhatsApp number, on WhatsApp) to say it missed the order and the item is still available. You are free to ignore it.

Points can be converted into money in your Levromart wallet at 100 points = ₦100, up to 100 points a day, and only points that are at least 24 hours old; otherwise they cannot be sold or transferred. Levromart may change or stop conversions at any time and may refuse or reverse conversions of points earned unfairly. Codes must be added within 7 days of creating an account, you cannot use your own code, and points from fake or self-referred accounts can be removed.
